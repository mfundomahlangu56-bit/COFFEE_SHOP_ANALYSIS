Data Analysis project focused on understanding coffee shop sales trends, customer behavior, and product performance.The company has appointed a new CEO whose mission is to grow overall revenue and improve product performance.As a junior data analyst,your role is to extract actionable insughts from the company's historical data and present key findings that will guide strategic decision making.
This project involves cleaning,analysing and  visualizing sales datato highlight trends,opportunities and performance gaps acrossproduts,time periods and regions
# OBJECTIVES
Identify revenue growth trends over time
Evaluate product performance and profitability.
Highlight top-performing  regions,stores or catagories.
Detect seasonal patterns or time-based sales trends.
Provide data-driven recommendations to support the CEO's revenue growth strategy.

----- REVENUE ANALYSIS
